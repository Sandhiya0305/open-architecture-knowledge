# Contributing to Open Architecture Knowledge

This repository is a curated reference, not an unreviewed feed of generated summaries. Contributions should improve accuracy, traceability, and practical decision-making.

## Before proposing a change

1. Identify the architecture concept and distinguish it from frameworks, libraries, and deployment products.
2. Prefer primary sources: specifications, official documentation, source repositories, and reproducible research.
3. Record the source URL and publication or retrieval date for time-sensitive claims.
4. Separate established architectural principles from adoption signals and empirical measurements.
5. Do not infer runtime performance from stars, downloads, mentions, or popularity.

## Evidence requirements

For a performance or benchmark claim, include:
- Workload and tested operations.
- Hardware and execution environment.
- Language, runtime, framework, and relevant dependency versions.
- Methodology, including repetitions and measurement approach.
- Results, units, and measurement date.
- Source link and known limitations.

If essential methodology is missing, label the claim **unverified** and do not use it to rank architectures.

## Architecture page checklist

- Definition and boundaries.
- Core characteristics and common variants.
- Strengths and trade-offs.
- Suitable use cases and warning signs.
- Relevant implementation patterns, without implying a framework enforces the architecture.
- Sources for factual or time-sensitive claims.

## Review and publishing

- Keep changes focused and explain why they improve the knowledge base.
- Preserve historical observations; date-stamp trend updates.
- Generated content must be reviewed by a human.
- Use pull requests. Do not automatically merge generated changes.
- Avoid copying substantial third-party text; summarize in original language and link to the source.
