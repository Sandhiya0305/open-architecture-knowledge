# About Open Architecture Intelligence

Open Architecture Intelligence (OAI) is an open-source effort to make software architecture knowledge easier to understand, compare, and maintain.

The project has two repositories:

- [open-architecture-intelligence](https://github.com/Sandhiya0305/open-architecture-intelligence) is the research engine. It discovers public information, stores observations, classifies candidates, assesses evidence, and prepares review material.
- [open-architecture-knowledge](https://github.com/Sandhiya0305/open-architecture-knowledge) is the curated knowledge source for this website.

## Principles

- **Evidence before certainty:** preserve sources and label uncertainty.
- **Trade-offs over hype:** explain costs as well as benefits.
- **Architecture is contextual:** recommendations depend on constraints and quality goals.
- **Separation of concerns:** distinguish architecture, frameworks, libraries, and deployment products.
- **Human accountability:** generated changes are proposals; people review and merge them.
- **Open and reproducible:** prefer public sources, transparent methods, and version history.

## Current scope

The initial architecture references cover layered architecture, modular monoliths, microservices, Clean Architecture, and event-driven architecture. The initial technology research scope includes Node.js, Python, Java, and PHP.

This is a starting scope, not a claim of exhaustive coverage.

## What OAI is not

OAI is not a universal architecture ranking, a benchmark leaderboard, or an automated authority that declares one pattern best. It does not turn popularity into performance claims. It does not publish unreviewed AI-generated research as established fact.
